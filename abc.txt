Arduino is an open-source electronics platform (microcontroller boards + 
software) used to build interactive projects that sense and control the
 physical world. Common uses:
    • Prototyping electronics and embedded systems
    • Learning electronics, programming, and physical computing
    • Controlling lights, motors, sensors, relays, displays, and servos
    • Home automation (lights, thermostats, security sensors)
    • Robotics (motor control, sensor fusion, navigation)
    • Wearables and interactive art installations
